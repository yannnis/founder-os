Design book

An Aegean country house in a warm, quiet register. The architecture is simple and white, so the interior leans on texture rather than colour: lime-plastered walls in a warm white, honed limestone underfoot downstairs and wide oiled oak upstairs, chestnut beams and a reed ceiling where the roof shows. Sculptural plaster forms the fireplace, the kitchen hood and the banquette. Linen, bouclé and wool bring softness; travertine and Calacatta Viola marble bring weight. Colour is used sparingly and comes from the island itself: terracotta, ochre, olive, and the blue of the sea through the glass.

The pool garden

Designed the way a good small hotel would do it: one long, calm body of water, everything a guest needs within a few steps of it, and nothing that shouts. The pool is 10 × 4.5 m and built deck-level, so the water is flush with honed limestone and spills into a slot grate. A 25 cm sun ledge runs along the sunny side with four in-water loungers, so they seem to float without taking any swimming water. A teak deck hovers on a recessed plinth with two daybeds turned to the sunset. The pool house hides the cars and holds a shaded lounge and a pantry for coffee, cold drinks and towels.

A day by the pool

  1. 08:00The sun comes up straight down the length of the water. Coffee from the pool pantry, taken to the two chairs at the far end by the old olive.
  2. 10:00Towels from the shelves in the pantry, the outdoor shower on green zellige, then in by the Roman steps at the house end.
  3. 12:00The sun ledge: lie in 25 cm of water on the in-water loungers, a drink on the little table between each pair, the parasol standing in the pool.
  4. 14:00Lunch at the pergola, then the shade of the pool house: linen mattresses, drapes, a reed ceiling, a nap.
  5. 18:30The daybeds, turned to the sunset with a carafe on the travertine table. The border north of the water is kept low so the evening sun reaches the pool.
  6. 21:00The pool glows from its side lights, an LED line lights the gap under the teak deck, the olives are lit from below, the fire pit is going on the north lawn.
